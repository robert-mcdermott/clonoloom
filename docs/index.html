Documentation

ClonoLoom opens the output of an annotation pipeline for T- and B-cell receptors, checks it, and groups it into clonotypes by a definition you can see. From there it measures each repertoire with its uncertainty and compares samples, keeping every step on record. Analysis runs on your computer; public queries and sequence handoffs require explicit review.

How it works

  • One program, on your computer. ClonoLoom reads local sources without changing or moving them. Uploads, exports and portable archives create separate copies when requested. Public-repository queries and reviewed sibling handoffs require explicit actions and the documented permissions; opening a view sends no local receptor sequences.
  • Checked before it is used. Data are checked before analysis, and the vendors' own numbers are recomputed from the rows. Each method is validated against an independent implementation or a known truth, with the numbers on the Science page.
  • The definition travels with the result. Every clonotype count, figure, export and methods paragraph states the clonotype definition and the abundance unit it was made under.
  • Everything is kept. Results are kept with their lineage, parameters, seeds and the SHA-256 of their files; asking again answers the result kept. Filters are kept as versions, and undo covers every change.

Four references stay in the repository: Formats, every file ClonoLoom reads and writes; the remote control reference, every action a script can send; simulated studies, the model and its truth; and installing. The changelog says what each release added.
