Immune repertoires you can check

ClonoLoom is a desktop workbench for researchers studying T- and B-cell receptor repertoires. Compare samples and study groups, measure diversity, track clones over time, explore B-cell families and mutations, and connect receptors with single-cell expression and cell states. Use it to investigate immune responses across individuals, experimental conditions and time—including vaccination, disease and treatment studies.

Turn your analyses into clear figures, shareable results and cited methods, with a record of the data and choices behind each result. ClonoLoom runs locally on macOS, Linux and Windows, with no account, Python or R required. Public-data queries and sequence transfers require your explicit approval.

Why ClonoLoom

Checked from the file up

Every file read by ClonoLoom's own readers and checked against independent ones; a validator that says what is wrong, in how many rows, and what to do; Cell Ranger's and immunoSEQ's own numbers recomputed from the rows.

Clonotypes by definition

A clonotype is whatever its definition says. The junction, the genes, the chains and the rules for single cells are a named choice, stated in words with every table, figure, export and methods paragraph.

Every number with its uncertainty

Diversity, gene usage and overlap each with a 95% interval, how often those intervals hold the truth measured on simulated repertoires; samples compared at equal coverage rather than as deep as they happened to be sequenced.

Private and on the record

One program for macOS, Linux and Windows. Analysis runs on your computer and leaves source files unchanged. Public queries and sequence handoffs require explicit review. Every result is kept with what it was made from, and a methods paragraph is written from that record.

What it does

From the annotation pipeline to results others can check

Open a study's receptor data, check them, look at each sample's quality, define clonotypes, measure the repertoires and compare them, and write it up so that others can check it, each step kept on record.

B cells

From sequence evidence to families and lineages

Inspect AIRR or joined IMGT annotations, choose a family threshold within subjects, reconstruct D-masked germlines from pinned references, and measure substitutions with explicit denominators. Explore parsimony trees with their ties and ancestral ambiguity; summarize observed constant calls with cell counts and unresolved calls retained.

  • Source rows, parent checksums and cited methods stay with each result
  • Full CSV/JSON tables, germline and lineage FASTA, and Newick tree exports
  • Artificial known truth checks bookkeeping; trees and co-occurrence do not establish switching events

Open and check

Data checked before they are used

ClonoLoom reads AIRR Rearrangement tables with their Repertoire metadata, Cell Ranger V(D)J outputs, immunoSEQ exports, MiXCR clone tables, OAS and OTS data units and tcrdist3 clone tables with its own readers, fingerprints every file by its SHA-256, and checks it: each finding fatal, a warning or a note, with how many rows it concerns, examples, and what to do. The abundance unit is stated, never guessed.

  • Every column of 25 development files as the airr library and pandas read it
  • Another tool's CSV or TSV through a mapping of its columns, checked column by column
  • A moved file found again by its fingerprint; a changed one never read in its place
Opening data
10x Genomics' PBMC 1k T cells checked: the format, the unit, the license, and one note.
Quality control

Each sample's quality, the vendor's numbers recomputed

The QC view measures every sample from its rows: the productive share, clonotypes, singletons, sample coverage, the largest clonotype's share and how well genes were called; for single cells, each cell's chains. Cell Ranger's and immunoSEQ's own numbers are recomputed beside them. Filters are saved as versions, each with its reason, and never change the data.

  • Cell Ranger's cell metrics reproduced exactly for six runs
  • immunoSEQ's clonality, entropy and largest frequency reproduced from its reads
  • Sample coverage equal to iNEXT's to 10⁻¹²
Quality control
10k PBMC T cells: cells by their productive chains, and the junctions' lengths.
Clonotypes

Clonotypes by a definition you can see

Choose a preset or edit a definition: the junction in nucleotides or amino acids, V and J by allele, gene, family or not at all, one chain or paired chains, and what to do with cells that have extra or missing chains. The definition is stated above every table and in the status bar, and each clonotype has an ID made from it, the same in every run.

  • Paired definitions equal to scirpy's partitions of cells on eight Cell Ranger runs
  • Cell Ranger's own clonotypes, or an AIRR file's clone_id, kept as a definition
  • A million clonotypes sorted and searched as they scroll
Clonotypes

Diversity and rarefaction

Diversity with its uncertainty, compared fairly

Hill numbers, Chao1, ACE, the Shannon and Simpson diversity of the repertoire a sample was drawn from, clonality, Gini and clonal space homeostasis, each with a 95% interval. Rarefaction and extrapolation show what each sample would hold at any size or coverage, and compare the samples at a level every one of them reaches.

  • Equal to iNEXT, immunarch and vegan on 18 samples
  • Curves within 10⁻⁹ of iNEXT's at its 40 sizes along each
  • How often the intervals hold a simulated truth, measured and stated
Diversity
Twelve samples compared at the lowest coverage among them, with their curves by size and coverage.
Genes and junctions

What the clonotypes are made of

Each sample's V, D, J and C genes and their families, V–J pairs, junction lengths and amino acids, overall and at each IMGT position; by clonotypes or by abundance, for each sample or each condition, with intervals from a bootstrap of the clonotypes.

  • 8,277 shares equal to an independent R implementation to 10⁻¹²
  • A call tied between genes shared between them, what was not called said
  • Intervals that held the simulator's truth in 97.4% to 100% of cases
Genes and junctions
Each sample's V genes, and one gene across the samples.
Overlap

Samples compared with each other

For every pair of samples: the clonotypes they share, Jaccard's and Sørensen–Dice's indices and the overlap coefficient, as sampled and rarefied to the same depth, and Morisita's index over their abundances. The pairs of one subject and of two are summarized, and the clonotypes found in more than one subject are counted.

  • Equal to immunarch, vegan and R on 69 pairs, and to scirpy on four single-cell runs
  • Intervals widened for the chance that decides which clonotypes two samples share
  • Those intervals held the truth in 95.2% of 330 simulated pairs
Overlap
Every pair of twelve samples by Morisita's index; the two samples of each subject stand out.
Tracking

Clonotypes followed through time, and tested

Each subject's samples placed in time from their annotation or metadata. Its clonotypes followed through the time points with their intervals, as threads and alluvial plots; tested between two time points by the beta-binomial test, its variance between blood samples fitted on replicate samples, with false discoveries controlled; classed across several; and each sample measured against an infusion product or a donor.

  • Every p-value equal to R's, or an independent R implementation's, to 4 × 10⁻¹²
  • No false call where nothing changes, where the binomial test makes some
  • A donor's share of the blood within 0.065 of the truth

Figures, exports and the record

Results others can check

Every plot is saved as a figure whose caption states the clonotype definition, the unit and the samples. Rearrangements, clonotypes and results are exported with a notice of where they came from and under what license. A methods paragraph is written from the project's record, with numbered references, saying only what was done.

  • Figures as SVG, PNG at 300 dpi or PDF
  • AIRR Rearrangement TSV and CSV, each with its data's license and citation
  • Every release's project opened again, and its results made again, by the next

And

Built to be checked, taught and scripted

Examples with known answers

Public 10x Genomics, AIRR Community and immuneACCESS data, each with its license and citation, and simulated TRB and artificial B-cell studies made on your computer, with known generating truth.

Scripts and notebooks

Programs on your computer drive the open window through remote control: open data, choose a definition, measure, and read the answers as JSON, each action in the project's history.

Any tool's table

VDJtools' or a spreadsheet's table, opened through a mapping of its columns that ClonoLoom guesses from their names and checks over the whole file before it opens.
