How methods are checked

Three kinds of check, as each method allows: the same numbers as an independent implementation of the same method on the same inputs (the airr library, pandas, scirpy, iNEXT, immunarch, vegan and R, run by scripts in the repository's validation/reference/ and their outputs committed); the truth of a simulated study, whose every clonotype, sample and expectation is known; and invariants worked by hand on small cases. Tools under licenses ClonoLoom cannot include are only run, never copied.

Reading and checking data

ClonoLoom reads AIRR Rearrangement TSV (schema 1.3 to 2.0; Vander Heiden et al. 2018) with its Repertoire metadata (Rubelt et al. 2017), Cell Ranger V(D)J contig annotations and output folders, immunoSEQ v2 and v3 exports, MiXCR 2, 3 and 4 clone tables, the data units of the Observed Antibody Space (Olsen et al. 2022) and the Observed T cell receptor Space (Raybould et al. 2024), and tcrdist3's clone tables (Mayer-Blackwell et al. 2021) with its own readers, into one columnar table. Every column of 35 development files equals what the AIRR Community's airr library 2.0.0 and pandas 3.1.0 read from them, and the SHA-256 each reader takes while reading equals each file's recorded checksum: every byte was read, and none was changed. The readers were fuzzed with 33 million inputs without a fault. Gene calls are kept in IMGT nomenclature (Lefranc 2014), Adaptive's names mapped to IMGT's by ClonoLoom's rules and the HGNC list of human TR and IG genes (Seal et al. 2023). A table of another tool is read through a mapping of its columns that the user confirms, checked over the whole file first. A million rearrangements are read and validated in 1.5 s, at 58 bytes a row and their junctions.

Quality control

On six bulk samples (immunoSEQ, the AIRR Data Commons and the AIRR Community's files), rows, abundance, productive share, clonotypes, singletons, doubletons and the largest clonotype's share equal an independent R implementation's, and sample coverage (Chao and Jost 2012) equals iNEXT 3.0.2's, to 10⁻¹². Cell Ranger's cell metrics (cells, cells with a productive pair, with a productive chain of each locus, as counts and shares) are reproduced exactly as Cell Ranger 4.0, 6.1 and 9.0 reported them for six T- and B-cell runs, and immunoSEQ's productive clonality, Simpson clonality, entropy and largest frequency from each rearrangement's reads, as Adaptive computes them, to seven significant digits. On the simulated standard study, each sample's estimated coverage is within 0.005 of the coverage a sample of its depth has on average.

Clonotypes

A clonotype definition names the junction (nucleotides or amino acids, the IMGT junction from the conserved cysteine at 104 to the phenylalanine or tryptophan at 118; Lefranc et al. 2003), the V and J genes at allele, gene or family level or not at all, the chains, and for single cells which chains of a cell count. On eight Cell Ranger runs (T and B cells, Cell Ranger 4.0, 6.1 and 9.0), ClonoLoom's paired definitions give exactly scirpy 0.25.1's partitions of cells (Sturm et al. 2020), by nucleotide and amino-acid junction, with and without the V gene; Cell Ranger's own clonotypes have the sizes its clonotypes.csv gives; bulk clonotypes equal the R implementation's on six samples under three definitions. A page of a million clonotypes, sorted and searched, comes in 37 ms.

Over 200 samples of a well-sampled simulated repertoire (coverage 0.99), the 95% intervals held the truth in 96.0% of samples for Chao1, 93.5% and 91.5% for the estimated Shannon and Simpson diversity, and 92.5% to 99.5% for the values seen. In an undersampled repertoire (coverage 0.62), Chao1 and the estimated Shannon diversity are lower bounds and their intervals never held the truth; the Repertoire view says so whenever a sample's coverage is below 0.9. iNEXT floors the estimated Simpson diversity's interval at the value seen; with that floor, the interval held the truth in under half the samples, so ClonoLoom does not apply it.

Rarefaction and extrapolation

Each sample's clonotypes, Shannon diversity and Simpson diversity (the Hill numbers of order 0, 1 and 2) and its coverage are rarefied below its size and extrapolated up to twice it (Chao et al. 2014), and the Hill numbers given at any coverage (Chao and Jost 2012). On 18 samples, at iNEXT 3.0.2's 40 sizes along each curve (Hsieh et al. 2016), every value equals iNEXT's to 10⁻⁹, except the extrapolated Shannon diversity, within 8 × 10⁻⁸: its extrapolation magnifies iNEXT's rounding of the value seen, which ClonoLoom computes to 10⁻¹⁵. Two cohorts compared at iNEXT's default levels give the same levels and values; the bootstrap standard errors are 0.85 to 1.26 times iNEXT's (median 1.01, over 4,240).

On simulated repertoires, rarefied to half a sample's size, every value's 95% interval held the truth in 90% to 98% of samples. Extrapolated to twice the sample, the values were within 3% of the truth on average, but where rare clonotypes abound only the Simpson diversity's intervals held the truth as often as they say. ClonoLoom therefore compares samples first at levels every sample reaches by rarefaction alone (the lowest coverage among them, or the smallest sample's size), and offers iNEXT's extrapolated levels second, with a warning.

Gene usage and junctions

Each sample's V, D, J and C genes and families, V–J pairs, junction lengths and their mean, and amino acids overall and at each IMGT position of the commonest lengths (Lefranc et al. 2003), by clonotypes or by abundance, with intervals from 200 bootstrap replicates of the clonotypes. A call tied between genes gives each an equal part. On 15 samples, all 8,277 shares equal an independent R implementation's to 10⁻¹²; against the simulator's truth, of 2,308 intervals, 97.4% to 100% held the expected shares.

The binomial and Fisher's tests account for sampling alone. When each clone's frequency in each blood sample also varies around its expectation (by a factor of mean 1 and variance 0.1), they called 13 of 6,599 clonotypes on the series where nothing changes (0.2%, as Rytlewski et al. found between blood samples two weeks apart), and 6 of the binomial test's 73 calls on the CAR-T study were against the truth (8%, against the 1% asked for). Where tcrdist3 and Dash et al.'s description differ, ClonoLoom does as tcrdist3 does: the CDR3's gap placed where it costs least, 12 for each residue of difference in length, and the gap character of the V genes' aligned loops costing nothing against a residue. The loops are tcrdist3's database of genes (made from IMGT's germline genes), downloaded on request and named by its checksum in every result. The neighbors of 100,000 paired TCRs (real α and β chains of three OTS donors, paired at random) are found in 25 seconds on 10 processors, within 0.1 GB.

A centroid's radius is the largest of 0, 2, …, 48 TCRdist units at which the weighted background within it is at most a share of the background's receptors (10−6 by default, Mayer-Blackwell et al.'s; tcrdist3's own default is 10−5), and the dataset's receptors within it are its neighborhood. The backgrounds are ClonoLoom's own: receptors drawn from a recombination model with the dataset's V–J usage, each weighted by the model's probability of its pair over the dataset's share of it, so that the weighted background is the repertoire recombination alone makes; or the dataset's own chains with their CDR3s moved among its genes. Because a radius is chosen to leave almost no background within it, a neighborhood's size is the signal, not its radius; enriched neighborhoods are ordered by descending χ², with depleted ones after them. Ten thousand receptors against a background of 100,000 take 1.3 seconds on ten processors.

ClonoLoom computes a CDR3's probability as OLGA does, summed over every gene choice, deletion, palindrome and insertion by dynamic programming over its codons, and with OLGA's choices: every insertion length the model allows, a palindrome made from the gene's part of the CDR3, and only the genes OLGA's anchor files mark functional. OLGA leaves out an insertion of the longest length a model allows when that length is one more than a multiple of three and the insertion starts a codon, which its own generator makes; ClonoLoom counts them, and is checked against OLGA on a copy of each such model that allows one nucleotide more with probability 10−30. Of the seven models, only mouse TRA's probabilities change by it by more than 10−15: by up to 13% among the 5,583 mouse TRA CDR3s compared. A million TRB CDR3s' probabilities take 32 seconds on 10 processors.

Simulated studies

clonoloom simulate makes TRB rearrangements from GRCh38's gene segments at Ensembl's annotation (Dyer et al. 2025), with V and J usage from 10x Genomics' 10k PBMC data, deletions, palindromic and non-templated insertions (Murugan et al. 2012), and productive junctions selected on their length (Elhanati et al. 2014); each subject's repertoire a population of power-law clone sizes, sampled into templates and reads. Over 150,000 rearrangements, the productive junctions' mean length is within 0.1 amino acids of the 10x data's, its standard deviation within 0.15, and V usage within 0.03 of the model's in total variation. The same seed gives the same bytes on every computer.

The vaccination study's receptors that recognize an epitope are families around founders the subjects share, each member the founder's genes and deletions with other non-templated nucleotides, and receptors of each subject's own. Their nearest receptors of the same epitope are as near as in Dash et al. 2017's epitope-specific β chains: 58%, 63% and 68% within 12, 24 and 48 TCRdist units, against 54–61%, 59–71% and 69–85% in Dash et al.'s human and mouse chains. Their share of each sample is within a quarter of the truth's at every time point.

Figures, exports and the record

Figures are written as SVG, as PNG at 300 dpi (the resolution recorded in the file) and as PDF; the PDF's structure, page size and image are checked by tests. An exported AIRR Rearrangement TSV, read back by ClonoLoom's own reader, gives the same clonotypes with the same abundances. Each release saves a project with its data and results, and every later release must open it under the same dataset and result IDs and make each result again: the 0.2.0 project's five results are made again with the same values. Results are the same, bit for bit, on every processor: ClonoLoom computes its own logarithms, exponentials, powers and log-gamma (fdlibm's, as SituCraft's), rounds every product on its own so that no processor fuses it with an addition, and a test searches the compiler's arm64 output for fused multiply-adds; every analysis's results, and a simulated study, are pinned by digests that arm64 and amd64 builds both give.

B-cell evidence, families and lineages

The artificial immunization study supplies known generating families, ancestors, mutation events and isotype labels. Independent checks read its truth and source files directly; they do not call the analysis implementation. These checks validate the stated computations on known artificial inputs, not biological sensitivity, an optimal family threshold or switching-event inference.

Public Ramos and 10x PBMC B-cell inputs test evidence coverage and explicit exclusions, not known biological ancestry. Each result pins its source and parent identities. A saved 0.5.0 project with paired AIRR and joined IMGT inputs reproduces all seven result documents byte for byte on arm64 and amd64, alongside the earlier release fixtures. Exact lineage search covers at most eight distinct states including the reference; the bounded 9–64-state heuristic does not certify a global optimum. Conditional ancestral states are not probabilities. Mutation frequencies are net differences, not historical rates or selection tests. Isotype pairs do not establish switching events, timing, direction, ancestral traits or rates. The linked method guides and each result's methods paragraph state these limits.

Single-cell expression and receptor links

ClonoLoom 0.6.0 keeps expression matrices separate from receptor data and joins exact barcodes within an explicitly selected sample. Independent SciPy, h5py, AnnData, MuData and Awkward fixtures check raw counts, sparse structure, annotations, missingness and modality-local identities. The artificial paired cell-state model has separate truth, deliberate unmatched observations and a measured zero-count cell; supplied Gaussian coordinates are illustrations, not inferred embeddings.

Computed maps use all-gene count normalization, log1p, variance-ranked features, centered unscaled PCA, exact Euclidean neighbors and seeded UMAP. Independent numerical references check feature moments, PCA, neighbors and fuzzy-graph weights; known-state recovery and map-quality checks supplement deterministic result hashes. Expression methods distinguishes published methods from ClonoLoom's feature-selection and initialization choices.

Phenotype summaries count distinct cells under recorded sample, definition and QC rules, with denominators checked by independent SQL tabulations. Clonotype modularity uses a binary induced expression graph and uniform size-matched null, with exhaustive or seeded Monte Carlo tails and Benjamini–Yekutieli correction. Paired receptor/expression comparisons use mean PCs, TCRdist, shared-chain exclusions and inclusive cutoff ties, with conditional hypergeometric tails and all-scale Bonferroni adjustment. SciPy references and exhaustive small controls check those calculations. Modularity and CoNGA-style comparisons record population and null assumptions; these tests do not remove cell-state confounding, prove antigen specificity or make cells biological replicates.

AnnData 0.12.6 and h5py 3.15.1 independently read and rewrite the export, checking integer precision, every sparse count, Unicode, missing labels and coordinate alignment. The complete study retains 360 input cells in its export, including the cell excluded from its computed map. Its 0.6.0 project fixture replays all five results and the AnnData binary with recorded source and parent provenance, alongside the unchanged earlier release fixtures.
