How methods are checked

Three kinds of check, as each method allows: the same numbers as an independent implementation of the same method on the same inputs (the airr library, pandas, scirpy, iNEXT, immunarch, vegan and R, run by scripts in the repository's validation/reference/ and their outputs committed); the truth of a simulated study, whose every clonotype, sample and expectation is known; and invariants worked by hand on small cases. Tools under licenses ClonoLoom cannot include are only run, never copied.

Reading and checking data

ClonoLoom reads AIRR Rearrangement TSV (schema 1.3 to 2.0; Vander Heiden et al. 2018) with its Repertoire metadata (Rubelt et al. 2017), Cell Ranger V(D)J contig annotations and output folders, and immunoSEQ v2 and v3 exports with its own readers, into one columnar table. Every column of 25 development files equals what the AIRR Community's airr library 2.0.0 and pandas 3.0.6 read from them, and the SHA-256 each reader takes while reading equals each file's recorded checksum: every byte was read, and none was changed. The readers were fuzzed with 12 million inputs without a fault. Gene calls are kept in IMGT nomenclature (Lefranc 2014), Adaptive's names mapped to IMGT's by ClonoLoom's rules and the HGNC list of human TR and IG genes (Seal et al. 2023). A table of another tool is read through a mapping of its columns that the user confirms, checked over the whole file first. A million rearrangements are read and validated in 1.5 s, at 58 bytes a row and their junctions.

Quality control

On six bulk samples (immunoSEQ, the AIRR Data Commons and the AIRR Community's files), rows, abundance, productive share, clonotypes, singletons, doubletons and the largest clonotype's share equal an independent R implementation's, and sample coverage (Chao and Jost 2012) equals iNEXT 3.0.2's, to 10⁻¹². Cell Ranger's cell metrics (cells, cells with a productive pair, with a productive chain of each locus, as counts and shares) are reproduced exactly as Cell Ranger 4.0, 6.1 and 9.0 reported them for six T- and B-cell runs, and immunoSEQ's productive clonality, Simpson clonality, entropy and largest frequency from each rearrangement's reads, as Adaptive computes them, to seven significant digits. On the simulated standard study, each sample's estimated coverage is within 0.005 of the coverage a sample of its depth has on average.

Clonotypes

A clonotype definition names the junction (nucleotides or amino acids, the IMGT junction from the conserved cysteine at 104 to the phenylalanine or tryptophan at 118; Lefranc et al. 2003), the V and J genes at allele, gene or family level or not at all, the chains, and for single cells which chains of a cell count. On eight Cell Ranger runs (T and B cells, Cell Ranger 4.0, 6.1 and 9.0), ClonoLoom's paired definitions give exactly scirpy 0.25.1's partitions of cells (Sturm et al. 2020), by nucleotide and amino-acid junction, with and without the V gene; Cell Ranger's own clonotypes have the sizes its clonotypes.csv gives; bulk clonotypes equal the R implementation's on six samples under three definitions. A page of a million clonotypes, sorted and searched, comes in 37 ms.

Over 200 samples of a well-sampled simulated repertoire (coverage 0.99), the 95% intervals held the truth in 96.0% of samples for Chao1, 93.5% and 91.5% for the estimated Shannon and Simpson diversity, and 92.5% to 99.5% for the values seen. In an undersampled repertoire (coverage 0.62), Chao1 and the estimated Shannon diversity are lower bounds and their intervals never held the truth; the Repertoire view says so whenever a sample's coverage is below 0.9. iNEXT floors the estimated Simpson diversity's interval at the value seen; with that floor, the interval held the truth in under half the samples, so ClonoLoom does not apply it.

Rarefaction and extrapolation

Each sample's clonotypes, Shannon diversity and Simpson diversity (the Hill numbers of order 0, 1 and 2) and its coverage are rarefied below its size and extrapolated up to twice it (Chao et al. 2014), and the Hill numbers given at any coverage (Chao and Jost 2012). On 18 samples, at iNEXT 3.0.2's 40 sizes along each curve (Hsieh et al. 2016), every value equals iNEXT's to 10⁻⁹, except the extrapolated Shannon diversity, within 8 × 10⁻⁸: its extrapolation magnifies iNEXT's rounding of the value seen, which ClonoLoom computes to 10⁻¹⁵. Two cohorts compared at iNEXT's default levels give the same levels and values; the bootstrap standard errors are 0.85 to 1.26 times iNEXT's (median 1.01, over 4,240).

On simulated repertoires, rarefied to half a sample's size, every value's 95% interval held the truth in 90% to 98% of samples. Extrapolated to twice the sample, the values were within 3% of the truth on average, but where rare clonotypes abound only the Simpson diversity's intervals held the truth as often as they say. ClonoLoom therefore compares samples first at levels every sample reaches by rarefaction alone (the lowest coverage among them, or the smallest sample's size), and offers iNEXT's extrapolated levels second, with a warning.

Gene usage and junctions

Each sample's V, D, J and C genes and families, V–J pairs, junction lengths and their mean, and amino acids overall and at each IMGT position of the commonest lengths (Lefranc et al. 2003), by clonotypes or by abundance, with intervals from 200 bootstrap replicates of the clonotypes. A call tied between genes gives each an equal part. On 15 samples, all 8,277 shares equal an independent R implementation's to 10⁻¹²; against the simulator's truth, of 2,308 intervals, 97.4% to 100% held the expected shares.

Morisita's own index is shown first: the Morisita–Horn index, which immunarch and vegan give, reads two samples of one repertoire about 0.98 when they are shallow, and its intervals held the simulated repertoires' overlap in 85.8% of pairs, against 93.9% for Morisita's. A bootstrap of the cells alone held the shared clonotypes in only 64% of pairs: which clonotypes two samples share is itself random, and the intervals now include that.

Wilson's interval is for sampling alone. When each clone's frequency in each blood sample also varies around its expectation, as in the simulated CAR-T study (by a factor of mean 1 and variance 0.1), the same intervals held the true frequency in only 76.9% of cases. The view and the methods paragraph say so, and the expansion tests account for that variance from replicate samples.

Simulated studies

clonoloom simulate makes TRB rearrangements from GRCh38's gene segments at Ensembl's annotation (Dyer et al. 2025), with V and J usage from 10x Genomics' 10k PBMC data, deletions, palindromic and non-templated insertions (Murugan et al. 2012), and productive junctions selected on their length (Elhanati et al. 2014); each subject's repertoire a population of power-law clone sizes, sampled into templates and reads. Over 150,000 rearrangements, the productive junctions' mean length is within 0.1 amino acids of the 10x data's, its standard deviation within 0.15, and V usage within 0.03 of the model's in total variation. The same seed gives the same bytes on every computer.

Figures, exports and the record

Figures are written as SVG, as PNG at 300 dpi (the resolution recorded in the file) and as PDF; the PDF's structure, page size and image are checked by tests. An exported AIRR Rearrangement TSV, read back by ClonoLoom's own reader, gives the same clonotypes with the same abundances. Each release saves a project with its data and results, and every later release must open it under the same dataset and result IDs and make each result again: the 0.2.0 project's five results are made again with the same values. Results are the same, bit for bit, on every processor: ClonoLoom computes its own logarithms, exponentials, powers and log-gamma (fdlibm's, as SituCraft's), rounds every product on its own so that no processor fuses it with an addition, and a test searches the compiler's arm64 output for fused multiply-adds; every analysis's results, and a simulated study, are pinned by digests that arm64 and amd64 builds both give.
