How it works

  • One program, on your computer. ClonoLoom is a single file with nothing else to install. It reads every file with its own readers, never changes, moves or copies your data, and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Checked before it is used. Data are checked before analysis, and the vendors' own numbers are recomputed from the rows. Each method is validated against an independent implementation or a known truth, with the numbers on the Science page.
  • The definition travels with the result. Every clonotype count, figure, export and methods paragraph states the clonotype definition and the abundance unit it was made under.
  • Everything is kept. Results are kept with their lineage, parameters, seeds and the SHA-256 of their files; asking again answers the result kept. Filters are kept as versions, and undo covers every change.
